On 2026-09-02 the trial with another x64 AVX-512 test, this time with the initialisation sequence of the indirect 64-bit sorting function (which is fundamentally hard to optimise) ended unsuccesfully again. It succeeded in the sorting tests, but it remained 3.3% slower than the conventional version, and performed even worse on sizes smaller than the main one of 6 GiB. Its reliance on still rather slow gather and scatter operations, and a lot of shuffles to offset the issues with hard to vectorise sequences in the code just do not offer much of a chance to optimise the code this way.
